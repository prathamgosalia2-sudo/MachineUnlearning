First-Order Engine

Utilizes Gradient Ascent combined with reference-guided KL-Divergence anchoring. Maximizes cross-entropy loss on the forget set while anchoring to a frozen baseline model to prevent catastrophic forgetting on retain data.

Second-Order Engine

Leverages empirical Hessian Influence Functions isolated strictly to the final classification head (`model.fc`). Performs Newton-style parameter subtraction to eliminate O(d³) full-network inversion bottlenecks.
